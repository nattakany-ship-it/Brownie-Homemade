## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a luxury artisanal confectioner ethos: restrained, decadent, and steeped in gastronomic craftsmanship. The aesthetic merges traditional French pâtisserie refinement with warm, contemporary digital commerce. 

- **Personality:** Tactile, dignified, indulgent, and uncompromisingly precise.
- **Audience:** Discerning culinary enthusiasts, luxury gift buyers, and dessert connoisseurs seeking boutique, small-batch baked confectionery.
- **Emotional Response:** Cravings heightened by elegance; a sense of prestige, sensory richness, and culinary pedigree.
- **Design Style:** Warm Editorial Minimalism paired with Tactile Luxe. Deep dark chocolate foundations ground the layout, illuminated by burnished bronze accents, generous breathing room, and ivory parchment backdrops.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from roasted cacao terroirs, caramelized sugars, and hand-finished bronze packaging foils.

- **Primary (`#2C1810` - Rich Ganache):** Deep roasted cocoa bean tone used for authoritative titles, primary button backgrounds, and strong foundational structures. A darker tint (`#1A120B` - Noir Intense) acts as the high-contrast ink layer for fine text and deep footer surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Burnished Gold):** An understated metallic accent for badges, awards, interactive focus states, hover underlines, and luxury metadata indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#6E3B27` - Praline Roast):** A warm, mid-tone espresso brown used for secondary buttons, interactive card outlines, and supportive icons.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2` - Crème Parchment):** The foundation canvas. Paired with `#F5EFEB` (Warm Butter Layer) for inset panels, table rows, and secondary cards, completely eschewing harsh digital whites.
- **Text & Status:** Text on light backgrounds defaults to `#1A120B`. Error feedback uses `#8A2424` (Crimson Reduction), and success states use `#3D5A3E` (Matcha Laurel).

## Typography

The typographic harmony balances high-fashion editorial display with effortless multilingual readability for Thai and Latin character sets.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Playfair Display`. Its classical transitions, sharp bracketed serifs, and high-contrast downstrokes mirror vintage European patisserie menus. Headlines must maintain relaxed vertical spacing to accommodate Thai vowel marks and tone accents comfortably without clipping.
- **Body & Functional UI:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its open counter forms, clean geometries, and modern proportioning ensure legible product descriptions, dietary notices, and ordering configurations at small sizes.
- **Thai Language Integration:** For Thai body copy, maintain `line-height` ratios of at least 1.6 to ensure upper tone marks (ไม้โท, ไม้เอก) and lower vowels (สระอุ, สระอู) remain uncrowded. Headline tracking remains neutral (`0` to `-0.01em`), while English micro-labels and badges leverage letter-spacing up to `0.08em` with text transformed to uppercase.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an unhurried, editorial broadsheet structure. Elements breathe with intentional white space, preventing visual fatigue and allowing food photography to anchor the experience.

- **Grid Systems:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with max-width `1320px`, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `3rem` (`48px`) exterior margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem - 6rem on marketing blocks) convey prestige. Card paddings scale from `space-md` on compact checkout drawers to `space-lg` on product showcases.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established organically via soft parchment stacking, fine bronze keylines, and amber-tinted ambient glows rather than harsh synthetic grey dropshadows.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF7F2` (Crème Parchment).
  - **Level 1 (Card / Container):** `#FFFFFF` layered over parchment with a subtle 1px border colored `#E8DFD3`.
  - **Level 2 (Dropdown / Popover):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an amber diffuse shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.08)`.
  - **Level 3 (Modal / Persistent Drawer):** Deep vignette backdrop overlay `rgba(26, 18, 11, 0.65)` layered with a high-elevation surface carrying `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.16)`.
- **Gilded Borders:** Delicate, 1px keylines in `#C5A059` at 30% to 50% opacity are used to frame featured items, sensory tasting notes, and seasonal badges.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are intentionally kept modest (4px to 12px) to reflect precise, square-cut artisanal brownie slices, vintage tin confectionery boxes, and stationery-grade gift tags.

- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius for crisp, tailored edges.
- **Cards & Modal Trays:** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius.
- **Hero Containers & Visual Showcases:** Maximum `0.75rem` (12px) corner radius. Fully circular pills are reserved strictly for circular icon triggers and numeric notification counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2C1810`, text `#FAF7F2`, 4px radius, font `label-md`. Hover state transforms background to `#44261A` with a micro-shift upward and subtle warm shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.15)`).
- **Secondary / Luxe Accent:** Transparent background with a 1px solid `#C5A059` border, text `#2C1810`. Hover transitions to a filled `#C5A059` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Pure typographic action with an animated 1px underline that expands from center using `#C5A059`.

### Product Cards
- Built on white or light-sand surfaces with a 1px `#EAE1D5` border. 
- Photography uses 1:1 or 4:5 ratios with high-contrast studio shots of sliced brownies showing fudgy interior textures.
- The card footer pairs the product title (Thai/English bilingual hierarchy) with price in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans` and a minimalist gold "+" action to invoke quick customization.

### Badges & Chips
- **Dietary & Origin (Cacao %, Single Origin):** Subtle parchment pill (`#F5EFEB`), dark espresso text (`#2C1810`), 1px border (`#E4D9CC`).
- **Signature / Award:** Gold foil treatment with background `#2C1810`, border `#C5A059`, and text `#C5A059` set in `label-sm`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#D8CBBF`, placeholder `#9E8E81`. Focus state shifts the border to 1.5px `#C5A059` with a subtle ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-finished aesthetic. Unselected state is bordered in `#B8A99B`; selected state fills with `#2C1810` featuring an ivory checkmark or gold inner dot.

### Special Boutique Components
- **Cacao Flavor Wheel / Profile Bar:** A horizontal segmented scale illustrating Bitterness, Fudginess, Sweetness, and Nutty notes using rich chocolate and gold fill indicators.
- **Luxury Gift Box Builder:** An interactive grid with custom ribbon selection, personalized calligraphy card previews, and wax-seal badge toggles.
